Website privacy

Clear information.
In your hands.

How this company website handles your enquiry details.

Quote enquiry form

The form on this website prepares a message in your browser. It does not submit your form entries to a website database or save them in browser storage. Clicking “Open email draft” passes the enquiry into your email application. You decide whether to send it.

Copying your enquiry

The “Copy enquiry” button copies your prepared message to your device clipboard. You can then paste it into an email or the linked Enterprise contact form.

Email and other websites

If you send an email, your email provider and the recipient process that message. The Hiirad Enterprise website has its own contact form and privacy information. Follow its privacy links before using those services.

Website delivery

This static website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The website code does not set analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may process connection information to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Questions

For questions about information you share with Hiirad, contact info@hiirad.net.
