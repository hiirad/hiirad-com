Who we are

Built on relationships.
Focused on possibility.

Hiirad Ltd brings together commerce, logistics, technology and international business development. Our work connects emerging opportunities with established markets, with London as our base.

We take a practical approach: understand the need, build the right relationship and develop a clear route forward. Each division has its own focus, connected by a shared interest in useful, lasting commercial value.

Whether the conversation begins with a product, a supplier, a digital idea or a new venture, we believe that clarity and trust come first.
