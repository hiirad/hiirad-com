Before you get in touch
A few useful answers.
What should I include in a wholesale quote request?
Include product links or specifications, quantities, your business name, delivery country and preferred timescale. Prices, minimum order quantities and availability are confirmed by quotation.
Can I enquire about international shipping?
Yes. Include collection and destination locations, parcel count, dimensions and total weight. Hiirad can discuss the available air, sea or road options for your shipment.
How do I introduce a partnership?
Choose the relevant division and describe your company, proposed collaboration, target market and the support or relationship you are seeking.
Does this form send my enquiry automatically?
No. It prepares a message for you to review and send through your email app. You can also use the online contact form on the Hiirad Enterprise website.
